# The menu: ten slides

A deck, a home page, and the first sentence on a call are doorways. A stranger walks
past with a minute to spare and either knows, without stopping, who it is for, what
they get, and what it costs, or they do not. The menu is ten slides, one question
each, in this order. One picture and one sentence per slide. If the user needs a
paragraph, they do not have the answer yet.

The book names the slides in its own voice. Use the names that match the user's
chosen voice; the questions are the same.

| # | Unfiltered | Filtered | The question | Template decks call it |
|---|------------|----------|--------------|------------------------|
| 1 | Person | Person | Who is this for? Name, face, story. If this slide has a pie chart on it, it is already for the mirror. | Target audience |
| 2 | Orgasm | Outcome | What does done look like, for them, in their words afterwards? | Unique value proposition |
| 3 | Penetration | Way in | How will you get in instead of standing outside? Who makes the first call, who the hundredth, what happens on the second date. Not "viral". | Go-to-market |
| 4 | Offer | Offer | What, exactly, is on the menu? Show it. If a stranger cannot tell from this slide alone, it is a paragraph, not a slide. | Product (and then eight slides on it) |
| 5 | Timing | Timing | Why now, and what do you know about now that someone who only reads about it does not? | Why now |
| 6 | Size | Size | How much will the person on slide one part with, and how many people are just like them? Small and real beats big and imaginary. | Market size, TAM/SAM/SOM |
| 7 | Parties | Parties | Why are you the right people, what is in it for you, and have you had any sales yet? If so, did both sides get what they wanted? | Team + traction, on separate slides |
| 8 | Competition | Competition | Who else is at this, how, and what can you learn from them? Being first is not the prize. | Competitive landscape |
| 9 | Your wet dream | The dream | If you had a magic wand, what does tomorrow look like, in its most ambitious, slightly embarrassing form? | Vision |
| 10 | Ask | The ask | What are you missing, and what would you do with it today, not in six months? The second question is the one that gets the money. | The ask |

## Building it with the user

- Build the deck from the ten questions, not from a template. One picture, one
  sentence per slide; in a text-only deck the picture is one line describing the
  image. One sentence means one, not four clauses chained with semicolons. Write it
  to `MENU.md` as ten lines, each with the step on the rails it came from.
- Slides the user still wants after the ten go in a marked appendix after slide ten,
  never between them.
- Point out any slide that answers none of the ten, say which question it is missing,
  and let the user decide whether it stays.
- Slide one is a person with a name. If the user gives you a segment, go back to
  `PEOPLE.md`.
- Slide seven includes the honest sales count. Zero strangers paid is a fact, not a
  weakness, if slide three says who will be asked and when.
- Slide ten always has both questions. Almost every deck forgets the second.
- Before anyone who loves the user sees it, it goes to one stranger. Record the
  stranger's reaction in `STRANGERS.md`; "how much?" followed by silence is the sound
  of real math.

## The solution-first check

The mistake in nine decks out of ten, and most home pages, and most first sentences on
a call: opening with the product. The screenshot, the architecture, the feature list,
before anyone has been told who it is for or what it does for them. The book's name
for it, in the unfiltered voice, is a dick pic: slide four sent first, with the
response a dick pic gets from almost everyone. In the filtered voice: the solution-
first opener. Either way, flag it, and reorder: person, outcome, way in, then offer.

Run the same check on landing pages, cold emails, and the first sentence of any
pitch. If it starts with what the thing does, it is for the passenger. Write the
version that starts with what the person is afraid of or wants to be seen doing, and
show both.
